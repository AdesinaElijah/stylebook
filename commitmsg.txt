Remove messaging, base revenue on completed bookings, tighten reviews

Messaging is removed at the product owner's request. The backend half was
self-contained, so it comes out as ten deleted files with no edits to anything
that remains. NotificationType.NEW_MESSAGE and NotificationPreferences
.messageEnabled are deliberately kept: existing notification rows reference the
enum value, and dropping the column would mean a schema change for no benefit.

Reviews now require a COMPLETED booking rather than merely CONFIRMED. Bookings
auto-confirm 45 seconds after being made, so the previous rule allowed a
customer to book and leave five stars a minute later having never attended.
Requiring COMPLETED means the appointment time has at least passed. Attendance
is still inferred from the clock rather than known — a real check-in step is
the correct fix and is not built.

The owner dashboard now totals completed bookings instead of recorded
payments, so the figure appears without the owner having to mark anything. It
is labelled "Revenue this month", not "Earned", because a booking is completed
by elapsed time and that is not the same as knowing the customer turned up and
paid. Mark Paid remains available for owners who want the payment method and
the emailed receipt; it is simply no longer the source of the dashboard number.
